# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing customer churn behaviour using the Telco Customer Churn dataset to identify retention trends, high-risk customer segments, and key business factors affecting customer loyalty.

The analysis helps understand why customers leave the platform and provides actionable business insights to improve customer retention and reduce churn.

---

## Tools Used
- Python (Data Cleaning & Analysis)
- Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
- DAX (KPI Calculations)

---

## Dataset
- Source: Telco Customer Churn Dataset from Kaggle

---

## Business Questions
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
- How does contract type affect customer retention?
- What impact do monthly charges have on churn?
- How does customer tenure influence retention?
- Which internet service category experiences higher churn?

---

## Key Insights
- Month-to-month customers show the highest churn behaviour.
- Two-year contract customers demonstrate the strongest retention performance.
- Churned customers have higher average monthly charges ($74.44).
- Customers within the first 0–12 months are more likely to leave.
- Retention improves consistently as customer tenure increases.
- Fibre optic users exhibit higher churn levels compared to DSL users.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Improve onboarding support for new customers.
- Encourage long-term subscriptions through offers and discounts.
- Review pricing strategies for high monthly charge customers.
- Improve service quality for fibre optic users.
- Implement targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customers.

---

## Business Impact
By implementing these strategies, the business can achieve:
- Reduced early-stage customer churn.
- Improved customer loyalty and retention.
- More stable recurring revenue through long-term contracts.
- Better customer satisfaction and service experience.
